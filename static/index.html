    Upload a PDF and ask it questions. Answers use only the document's text, and the passage behind each one sits right beside it. If the document doesn't say, you'll be told so instead of a guess.

    Your PDF is never saved to disk. Its text is kept in memory only while you use it, and is deleted when you remove it or after 2 hours of inactivity. To write each answer, your question and the most relevant passages are sent to the AI model provider (Groq).
